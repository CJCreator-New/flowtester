# UX/UI review: landing page and Check-up flow

Status: review only, 2026-10-16. No source, test, spec, ADR or config was changed. Companion to [landing-and-home-spec.md](landing-and-home-spec.md); the look stays Blueprint ([DESIGN.md](../../packages/wizard/DESIGN.md)).

## 1. Scope, method, evidence legend

**Scope.** The landing page (`/`), the new Check-up screen (`/check`), scan (`/check/scan`), Plan Review (`/check/plan`), testing (`/check/testing`), the report (`/reports/:id`) and Past check-ups (`/reports`). Also the screens a person hits on the way: Connection, Not found, the top bar, the step bar, and the stop dialogs. Out of scope: Settings, Benchmark, Visibility, Visual Baselines, backend, checkers, scoring, the verdict.

**Method.** Code read, not rendered. Every finding comes from reading the screen's source, the flow wiring in `App.tsx` (`addressForPhase`, `reconcile`, `onEvent`, `stepOf`, `start`, `stopScan`, `stopTesting`, `approve`), the tokens in `index.css` and `tailwind.config.js`, and the rules in `CONTEXT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, ADR 0010, 0018, 0022 and the spec. Large files were read by range:

- `NewCheckupScreen.tsx`: 81-830 (form body, `canStart`, submit, `ResumeCard`), 1014-1090 (`AccessChoice`), 1313-1395 (`SignInFields`, `ConsentNotice`).
- `ReportScreen.tsx`: 57-630 (page order, header, actions bar), 847-920 (`Problems`), 1083-1215 (coverage, roles, sources).
- `PlanDocument.tsx`: 684-880 (`SummarySection`, `QuestionsSection`), 1807-1875 (`Overview`), 2019-2071 (section order). `PlanReviewScreen.tsx`, `ScanningScreen.tsx`, `TestingScreen.tsx`, `PastCheckupsScreen.tsx`, `ConnectionScreen.tsx`, `NotFoundScreen.tsx`, `LandingScreen.tsx`, `TopBar.tsx`, `StepBar.tsx`, `RunStates.tsx`, `router.ts`, `faq.ts`, `onboarding.ts`: whole.
- `packages/runner/src/session/checkup-run.ts` 855-896 only, to confirm what `skipReview` does.

**Evidence legend.** Each finding has `file#symbol` plus two marks.

- **confirmed**: the behaviour or text was read in code. **likely**: inferred (for example from class sizes or from a state that persists on the server).
- **markup**: judged from markup and tokens, not from a rendered page. **rendered**: none. No screenshots were taken, because the claims checked here (order, labels, claims, wiring) are settled by the markup. Pixel sizes on a 375 px phone are estimates from Tailwind classes and are marked likely.

Not read: `public/sample-report.html` (generated output, blocked for this review) and the JSON-LD in `index.html` beyond confirming that `FAQPage` and `HowTo` blocks exist there (`index.html` lines 125 and 133 carry the FAQ question text).

**Priority.** P0: a false claim on a safety promise, or it blocks the goal. P1: fix in the next UI pass. P2: polish or consistency.

**Fix rules used throughout.** Blueprint tokens and existing classes only (`btn-primary`, `btn-quiet`, `btn-link`, `blueprint-card`, `field`); no new token; 44 px targets; contrast stays inside the pairings `tests/contrast.test.ts` covers; verdict and scoring untouched (presentation only); Plan Review approval is never blocked; no recommendation weakens or hides a safety touchpoint; copy blocks avoid every `_Avoid_` term in `CONTEXT.md` and every ADR 0018 word. A copy change on a safety touchpoint carries a **SAFETY COPY** flag naming the touchpoint. Any change to `faq.ts` text names the `index.html` JSON-LD block as well.

## 2. Top findings

1. **F-09 (P0)** With sign-in details and consent, the wizard skips Plan Review by default, while the page still says "Nothing is tested until you approve the plan." SAFETY COPY.
2. **F-10 (P1)** The words "safe" (ADR 0018) and "staging" (Test Copy `_Avoid_`) sit on the Access choice and the live-site confirmation, the screens that guard the read-only default. SAFETY COPY.
3. **F-34 (P1)** On a 375 px phone the sticky top bar wraps to about 150 px, plus a breadcrumb row, a step bar and, on Plan Review, a second breadcrumb. About a third of the first screen is navigation.
4. **F-27 (P1)** The report pts three information cards between the verdict and "Problems found".
5. **F-28 (P1)** A second pass/fail ("Passed"/"Breached"), computed in the browser, sits beside the verdict stamp and can disagree with it.
6. **F-39 (P1)** Back from the report or from testing does nothing visible: `reconcile` bounces it forward. Stopping a scan leaves a dead address in history.
7. **F-01 (P1)** The one path from landing to scan uses three verbs for starting: "Run a free check-up", "Scan the site", "Start a new check-up".

**F-08 proposed copy.**

```
What is Release check-up?
Release check-up is QA for teams without a QA person. It explores a web app, writes a test plan for you to approve, tests it in a real browser and tells you in plain words whether the app is ready to release. It covers how it works, accessibility, speed, security and search readiness.

Where does my check-up happen, and who can see it?
On the free online copy, the check-up happens on our server, one at a time, and only public sites can be checked.
Your AI key and any sign-in details are kept in memory for your visit only, up to 24 hours, and never written to disk.
Each visitor sees only their own check-ups and reports, but everyone shares one server, so don't check anything you would not want on shared hardware. To check a site on your own computer or network, use Release check-up on your own machine, and nothing leaves it.
```
